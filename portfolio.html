What I built

Closed-loop attribution: Salesforce to Google Ads

+32% trainable revenue · $591K → $781K · 111 wins matched

Built a combined web-plus-phone click-ID so Google Ads could learn from phone sales, not just web checkouts. 69% of the new signal came from phone deals that a standard field mapping would have dropped. Stress-tested 22 of 22 cases before go-live.

Salesforce · Google Ads offline conversions · GA4

One-click quote to checkout

~30 minutes → 1 click

A sales console that drafts the quote with AI, generates the PDF, charges the card, sends the receipt and hands the order to shipping in one step. Every path that could double-charge a card or book a sale twice is closed: retries, timeouts, accounting outages, two contacts at one buyer.

Claude API · Stripe · QuickBooks · ShipStation · Salesforce

Lead routing and the follow-up board

$150,730 in live quotes put back into follow-up

Fixed routing that defaulted 87% of 1,395 inbound leads to one rep, rerouted by source, and traced a recurring reassignment loop to a third-party app. Rebuilt the rep follow-up board so all 143 quoted opportunities show, up from 50.

Salesforce · lead source data · third-party app audit

Order-to-cash automation

4 to 6 hours a day of manual entry eliminated

Auto-matched 5,526 web product variations to the accounting system, built hourly payment reconciliation across Stripe and QuickBooks, a duplicate-deal detector ($15,884 caught), and monitors that alert when a scheduled job silently dies.

Python · QuickBooks API · Stripe · WooCommerce · scheduled jobs
