How I sell

Consultative and application-first: what the buyer is weighing, the capacity and resolution they need, the platform, and whether the use is legal-for-trade. I built the tools I sell with: an AI-drafted quote-to-checkout console, an 11-step follow-up cadence, a follow-up desk ranked by deal value, and a practice tool trained on 3,009 real sales calls. Mining those calls showed lost large deals stalled on financing far more than on price or lead time, which changed how those conversations open.

Earlier I wrote the sales playbook for a FINRA-member broker-dealer and trained its team to a 20% higher close rate.

Method

Closed-won deals and quotes from the CRM; calls from the phone system; the benchmark is the company's top rep, identity withheld. Every figure traces to a closed opportunity, a quote or a logged call. I'm happy to walk through the numbers on a call and connect you with references.
