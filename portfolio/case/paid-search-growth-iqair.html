Case studies / Paid search and ecommerce growth

Grew paid search revenue 1,008.65% for an air-quality technology manufacturer

Paid search at IQAir, an air-quality technology manufacturer, was converting at 0.26%. After I restructured the program and its measurement, paid search revenue rose 1,008.65% and transactions grew from 521 to 2,323 against the prior period, while email revenue rose 93.00%.

The problem

Paid search converted at 0.26%, with 521 transactions over the prior comparison period. To grow it, the program had to be measured on orders and revenue by channel.

What I built

I restructured the paid search program and put conversion tracking in place so results could be read in Google Analytics as transactions and revenue by channel, not clicks.

I ran email programs in HubSpot alongside paid search and reported each channel on its own Google Analytics comparison window, including organic search.

Decisions that mattered

  • I judged paid search on transactions and revenue in Google Analytics rather than on traffic, which is why conversion rate is the headline alongside revenue.
  • I reported each channel against its own prior period so paid search, email and organic results were not blended together.

How the result was checked

All figures come from Google Analytics channel exports. Paid search compares Oct 19, 2015 to Jan 15, 2018 against Jul 21, 2013 to Oct 18, 2015. Email compares Jan 1, 2016 to Jan 15, 2018 against Dec 16, 2013 to Dec 31, 2015, where users also rose 117.08%. Paid search users rose 85.38% and organic search revenue rose 22.77%.
