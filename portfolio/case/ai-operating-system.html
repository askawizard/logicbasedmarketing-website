Built a 22-agent AI operating layer in 8 working days

Company knowledge sat in 370+ scattered documents, phone calls had no analytics, and most order steps were manual across disconnected systems. In 8 working days I shipped a 24/7 AI sales chat agent, a structured knowledge base, call analytics on 1,384 calls, a restored order sync on both stores and a 22-agent operating layer.

A B2B industrial equipment company selling by web, phone and dealers, running two WooCommerce stores, a phone-heavy sales process and several disconnected systems. I built this solo with Claude. Designed and built everything solo with Claude, in 8 working days.

The problem

Website visitors outside business hours had no path to a qualified conversation. 370+ documents across Word, Excel, PDF and email held the company's knowledge with no central system. 1,384 monthly calls produced no performance data, the store-to-shipping sync was down on both stores, and 16 of 17 steps in a phone sale were manual, with the same data entered into 5 to 6 systems per order.

What I built

I built an autonomous sales chat agent on Claude that handles website chat 24/7 with six tools: qualification capture, live availability, direct booking, human transfer, tech-support alerts and a bail-out alert for questions it cannot answer. I rewrote it from an 11-state scripted machine to a 3-state AI design, fixed 13 production bugs across the two versions, and covered it with 25 automated test scenarios. UTM attribution carries from the website through chat to the booking.

I synthesized 370+ source documents into 12 structured, AI-queryable files totaling 225KB, with a router file so any person or agent can find the right one. I wrote a Python extractor that pulled 1,384 calls from RingCentral and produced the company's first call benchmarks: inbound calls ran 47% longer than outbound and 34% of inbound calls passed 5 minutes. I restored the WooCommerce to ShipStation sync on both stores, so 5,515 orders now flow automatically.

On top of that I built an operating layer of 22 specialized agents across 8 departments, each with a defined mission, scoped knowledge access, handoff rules and tool permissions, plus a maintenance agent that audits the structure. I also connected and documented 11 API integrations.

Decisions that mattered

  • I replaced the 11-state scripted chat flow with a 3-state design where Claude decides what to ask and when to book, so the AI decides from the conversation instead of following a fixed script.
  • I gave every agent a scoped slice of the knowledge base instead of all of it, so each one works from the context its job needs.
  • I used a plugin the company already owned for the accounting sync instead of buying another tool.
  • I routed order-status questions, frustrated customers and broken-equipment issues straight to people rather than letting the bot handle them.

How the result was checked

The chat agent runs against 25 automated test scenarios drawn from real conversation patterns, and 13 production bugs were logged and resolved. Call benchmarks come straight from 1,384 records pulled through the RingCentral API. The order sync was confirmed by 5,515 orders syncing across both stores.
